Item 1. Business
Our Company. NVIDIA pioneered accelerated computing to help solve the most challenging computational problems. NVIDIA is now a full-stack computing infrastructure company with data-center-scale offerings that are reshaping industry. Our full-stack includes the foundational CUDA programming model that runs on all NVIDIA GPUs, as well as hundreds of domain-specific software libraries.

Item 1A. Risk Factors
The following risk factors should be considered in addition to the other information in this Annual Report on Form 10-K. Our business could be harmed by changes in export controls. The U.S. government has imposed licensing requirements on exports of certain of our data center products to China, which could reduce our revenue and help competitors that are not subject to the same restrictions.
